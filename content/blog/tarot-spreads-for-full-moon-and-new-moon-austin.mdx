From watching the night sky over Lady Bird Lake to spending quiet evenings out in the Hill Country, living in Austin connects us to the natural rhythms of the earth. 

Long before modern calendars were invented, human beings tracked time, agriculture, and emotional health by the phases of the moon. Combining **lunar cycles with tarot reading** creates a gentle, recurring rhythm for personal reflection, goal setting, and emotional check-ins.

Whether you are an experienced reader or just starting out with your first deck, working with the **New Moon** and **Full Moon** provides two natural anchor points each month to pause and calibrate your life.

## The New Moon: Planting Seeds & Setting Intentions

The New Moon occurs when the moon sits between the earth and the sun, rendering its face dark in the night sky. In symbolism and astrology, the dark moon represents **blank slates, fertile soil, and new beginnings**.

This is the ideal time for setting intentions, brainstorming fresh projects, and getting clear on what you want to invite into your life over the next month.

### The 4-Card New Moon Spread
Lay out these four cards during the night of the New Moon:

1. **Card 1: The Seed:** What intention or goal is ready to be planted in my life?
2. **Card 2: The Soil:** What resources or personal strengths will nourish this intention?
3. **Card 3: The Weeds:** What distraction or doubt do I need to clear out of the garden?
4. **Card 4: First Action:** What small, tangible step should I take during this waxing cycle?

## The Full Moon: Illumination & Releasing What No Longer Serves

Two weeks after the New Moon, the moon reaches its full illumination, glowing brightly in the night sky. The Full Moon represents **peak energy, fruition, heightened intuition, and emotional exposure**.

Just as the bright moonlight reveals objects hidden in the dark, a Full Moon tarot reading brings subconscious emotions, hidden truths, and outdated habits into clear view so you can let them go.

### The 4-Card Full Moon Spread
Lay out these cards under the light of the Full Moon:

1. **Card 1: What Has Harvested?** What progress or lesson has come to fruition over the past two weeks?
2. **Card 2: What Is Illuminated?** What hidden truth or blind spot is the full moon lighting up for me?
3. **Card 3: What to Release?** What boundary, resentment, or habit am I ready to release into the night?
4. **Card 4: Integration:** How can I bring my mind and spirit into balance as the moon begins to wane?

## Creating Your Personal Lunar Tarot Space

You don't need elaborate tools to create a meaningful lunar tarot practice. Simply set aside 15 minutes in a quiet spot:

- Light a beeswax candle or burn a small sprig of sage.
- Step outside or sit near a window to connect with the night sky.
- Keep a dedicated notebook to record your cards and thoughts so you can track how your intentions manifest over time.

Aligning your goals with natural cosmic cycles helps prevent burnout and reminds us that life moves in seasonal chapters.
